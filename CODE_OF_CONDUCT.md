# Code of Conduct

SpartanX adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming, respectful and constructive, in any language.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/xX-VyrnCore-Xx/SpartanX/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct"
or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
content and restrict participation, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
